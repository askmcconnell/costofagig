## Methodology Notes

1. **Best available retail price** used for each year, not necessarily lowest possible
2. **10-pack pricing** used where available for consistency with Elephant baseline
3. **Radio Shack pricing** is typically higher than discount mail-order — noted where applicable
4. **DS/DD floppy** always tracked as the historical baseline format regardless of whether it was the cheapest option
5. **Inflation adjustment** not applied — all prices are nominal retail at time of purchase
6. **Canadian prices** noted separately — not used in primary dataset
